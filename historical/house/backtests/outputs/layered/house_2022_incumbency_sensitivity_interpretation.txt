2022 House Incumbency Sensitivity Interpretation
================================================

Exploratory range tested:
0.00 to 3.00 points in 0.25-point increments.

Key results:
- Best MAE: 2.00-point incumbency bonus
- Best RMSE: 2.25-point incumbency bonus
- Broad performance plateau: approximately 1.75 to 2.50 points
- Existing production value: 1.50 points

Decision:
Retain the 1.50-point incumbency bonus as the official benchmark pending
validation on additional election cycles.

Reason:
The 2022 results suggest a somewhat larger effect, but optimizing the
coefficient on one election would create in-sample overfitting. The difference
between 1.50 and the 2022 optimum is small.

Probability-metric caution:
Brier score and log loss should not be used to select the incumbency
coefficient until the margin-to-probability error scale has been calibrated.

Candidate value for future multi-cycle testing:
2.00 points.
